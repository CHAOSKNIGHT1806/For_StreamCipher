## 1. Introduction and Related Work

Cellular-automaton (CA) stream ciphers trace back to Wolfram's rule-30 PRG (CRYPTO 1985) and were broken by Meier--Staffelbach (EUROCRYPT 1991, correlation-immunity failure) and Koc--Apohan (1997, low nonlinearity). The authoritative survey Mariot 2024 (arXiv:2405.02875) concludes rule 30 is *completely useless* for cryptography and catalogs the CA attack surface (Table \ref{tab:ca_attacks}).

**Known CA attack surface** (`ca_attack_surface.csv/.tex`):

- linear rule degeneration / permutivity -> correlation-immunity failure [Meier-Staffelbach, EUROCRYPT 1991, doi:10.1007/3-540-46416-6_17]
- low nonlinearity -> best-affine-approximation attack [Koc-Apohan, 1997 (per Mariot 2024, arXiv:2405.02875)]
- tap / center-cell sampling leakage -> neighbor correlation persists [Mariot 2024; Spencer 2013, arXiv:1306.3546]
- periodic boundary condition -> state collapse / non-invertibility [Wolfram 1985; Mariot 2024]
- low algebraic degree / immunity -> Berlekamp-Massey / Ronjom-Helleseth algebraic attacks [Mariot 2024, arXiv:2405.02875]
- relying on statistical/chaos tests as security -> necessary but not sufficient [Mariot 2024]
- poor diffusion -> slow differential propagation [Mariot 2024]
- fault injection -> FIA against CA stream cipher [Carrijo-Nascimento (Semantic Scholar)]
- asynchronous-update instability -> properties must be re-verified [Mariot 2024 (Manzoni 2018)]
